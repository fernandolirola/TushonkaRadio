# Changelog

## 1.0.15

- YouTube audio cache defaults to 100 MB, including preloaded tracks. Adjust the limit and see usage in F12.

## 1.0.14

- Optionally preload the next YouTube track while the current one plays. Enable it in F12 under YouTube.

## 1.0.13

- YouTube tracks use smaller audio downloads when available, reducing wait time and disk usage.

## 1.0.12

- Fixed another startup conflict with mods that register custom item types.

## 1.0.11

- Fixed a startup error when using Tushonka Radio with LoadAmmoAnim.

## 1.0.10

- YouTube playlists and videos use their original title when you leave the name blank.

## 1.0.9

- Clear downloaded YouTube tracks from the F12 settings menu.

## 1.0.8

- YouTube playback works without installing Node.js or downloading a separate tool.

## 1.0.7

- Hideout Radio is now called Tushonka Radio.
- The player opens with your last played YouTube playlist selected.
- Drag the corner to resize the player in either direction.

## 1.0.6

- Adjust music volume from the compact player.

## 1.0.5

- Toggle shuffle and repeat from the compact player.
- Drag the player to move it or resize it.

## 1.0.4

- Fixed the RADIO button sometimes failing to open the player.

## 1.0.3

- Reduced background work while the player is hidden or paused.

## 1.0.2

- YouTube audio setup now runs automatically when the game starts.

## 1.0.1

- Added RADIO to the native bottom menu beside HIDEOUT/CraftQueue, with F8 as the default shortcut.
- Replaced the large window with a compact player showing cover art, title, artist and playback controls.
- Matched the charcoal, orange and cream colors used by the other mods.
- Changed YouTube playlists to audio-only playback that continues when the player is closed or minimized.
- Added embedded/local cover art and song metadata for local files.

## 1.0.0

- Added YouTube playlists and local music playback in the menu and hideout.
- Added separate menu/hideout selections, saved playlists, volume, shuffle and repeat.
- Added automatic raid pausing and optional resume afterward.
- Added stock menu music muting while the radio plays.
- Added an F9 shortcut, Radio button, and English/Portuguese interface.
